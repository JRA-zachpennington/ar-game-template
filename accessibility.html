Accessibility

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Elf & Seek is a grocery-aisle hide-and-seek game for phones. We want every player to be able to read the screen, reach every control with a keyboard, and hear a screen reader describe what is on the page. We are working toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA (the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines).

Report a barrier

If something was hard to read, hard to reach, or did not work for you, email %ACCESS_EMAIL%. To help us find it quickly, please tell us:

  • which screen you were on (for example, the home screen or the hunt);
  • what you were trying to do;
  • what happened instead.

We aim to reply within 10 business days.

What we have done

This release improves the game's accessibility in specific ways:

  • the version stamp and the "EST. IN YOUR IMAGINATION" caption now meet the 4.5:1 contrast minimum on both the elf and unicorn skins;
  • the home brand link is announced with its full name and subtitle, and a "Skip to content" link is the first keyboard stop on every screen with the site header;
  • the home preview has a pause button, and it stops while a dialog is open;
  • the hunt caption sits on a dark plate so it stays readable over a bright camera image, and the discovery counter is announced as a labelled group;
  • each screen has a single top heading, dialogs are named by the heading you can see, and each trail-briefing picture has a text description;
  • the briefing hint and the celebration fireworks now finish on their own, and a keyboard and screen-reader pass covered the home screen, check-in, the trail briefing, the hunt, and the victory screen.

Known limitations

  • The camera and location prompts belong to your browser, not to the game.
  • The hunt needs the camera and location sensors to work.
  • Marker tracking is provided by a third-party library.
  • The home preview keeps moving until you pause it.
  • Turning on your device's "reduce motion" setting stops decorative animation.
  • A wrong tap in the trail briefing is answered only by a shake and the line played again; the words do not change.
  • Indoor GPS can be uncertain, so the location check may need a retry.
  • Pinch-to-zoom stays off so a pinch does not move the camera or the page.

We do not use an accessibility overlay or widget. This statement describes the real state of the game.
