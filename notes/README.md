# Printable trail codes

These are the seven AR barcodes the hunt recognizes. The game loads the same images from `public/markers/`.

Open `printable-codes.html` in a browser and print at **100% scale**, one code per letter-size page. Keep a white border around each black square. Do not crop or stretch the code.

| File | Barcode | Discovery | Hiding clue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `1.png` | 1 | Moon chip (cookie) | Look for a marker near the start of the host’s trail. |
| `2.png` | 2 | Berry button (cookie) | A splash of color may be hiding this cookie’s marker. |
| `3.png` | 3 | Honey star (cookie) | Check a low, easy-to-reach spot along the host’s trail. |
| `4.png` | 4 | Pip, the trail keeper | Pip likes a place where leaves make a little shade. |
| `5.png` | 5 | Clover, the garden dreamer | Clover feels at home near a pot or a garden bed. |
| `6.png` | 6 | Bramble, the cookie keeper | Bramble prefers a cozy place to sit and rest. |
| `7.png` | 7 | Ember, the lantern lighter | Ember waits near a welcoming doorway, away from the road. |

Hide the printed codes flat, uncovered, and well lit, on the host’s property and away from the road.
